Owner approval is not represented here as an independent copyright,
public-domain, trademark, privacy, or publicity-right determination for every
asset or every publication context.

For external, investor, customer, or public use, still identify the original
work and creator for the selected image and record the source URL, exact credit,
license or public-domain basis, adaptations, and retrieval date. NASA-origin
does not by itself prove that every contributor's credit or third-party right
is optional, particularly for joint NASA/ESA/CSA imagery. The independently
reviewed backgrounds above remain documented alternatives.

## Publication rule

The sample presentation contains a visible credit slide and source notes. If a
project deletes either default background, remove its credit. If it retains or
adds imagery, preserve the complete credit in a visible location wherever the
source, license, or publication policy requires it, and record the source,
rights basis, changes, and retrieval date here or in a project-specific
successor file.
