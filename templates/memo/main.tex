\section{Recommendation}

Approve a combined approach: use analysis to cover the broad operating space,
then test the boundary cases with the highest model uncertainty. This is an
illustrative decision structure; replace every claim, owner, and date with
project evidence.

\begin{LSKeyPoint}[Decision in one sentence]
Fund the smallest test campaign that invalidates the dominant assumptions,
while retaining analysis for coverage that is impractical to test directly.
\end{LSKeyPoint}

\section{Why This Is the Center View}

Analysis-only is fast but fragile where model-form uncertainty dominates.
Test-only is tangible but cannot economically span every combination of
temperature, rate, radiation, and life. The combined plan forces both methods
to challenge one another.

\section{Evidence Required Before Approval}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Rank assumptions by sensitivity and uncertainty.
  \item Map each acceptance criterion to analysis, test, inspection, or
  demonstration evidence.
  \item Identify test boundaries, instrumentation uncertainty, success criteria,
  owner, schedule, and configuration baseline.
  \item Record the residual risk and the authority accepting it.
\end{enumerate}
